## What is and is not here

- These are search results and fetched primary documents, not a magisterial
  synthesis. The author is not a theologian; the document is an engineering
  philosophy that cites the tradition and must be corrected by people who know
  it better.
- The synthesis is `../071-alignment-jesus-principle.md`.
- The executable contract is `../../docs/ALIGNMENT-ARMOR.md` and
  `src/wubu_armor.c`.
- Nothing here claims that a C module makes an AGI good. The module checks
  declared fields; it cannot check a heart, and it cannot detect a system that
  lies in the fields. That limit is stated in the contract itself.

## 2. What the order reveals

The lineage moved in a consistent direction:

1. **Identity** (who we are: WaefreBeorn, ~ WuBu ~) →
2. **Ownership** (what the work is, who may use it) →
3. **Governance** (how the rules change without decaying) →
4. **Alignment** (why the rules exist at all).

G39 is the hinge: it is the first document that treats a *rule change* as the
dangerous event. Its rule — a contract expansion needs a suite gain **and** no
golden-suite regression — is a constitutional principle in miniature: the floor
survives the frontier.

## 3. The licence's own political philosophy

The licence is not a generic permissive text. It encodes a three-tier polity:

| tier | definition | treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **The Populace** | individuals, sole proprietors, non-profits, educational, **government entities**, and any entity under 100 people **and** under $1M revenue | free |
| **Corporation** | not Populace, not Mega | paid terms |
| **Mega Corporation** | any two of: >1,000 FTE, >$100M revenue, publicly listed | restricted |

That is **subsidiarity with thresholds**: the small and the public keep
authority; the giant must negotiate. It is also **solidarity**: the weak get the
same work at no cost. And it is **non-domination**: the point of the Mega tier
is not revenue but preventing capture.

The preamble states the anthropology outright: *"The populace is free. The
corporation is an entity of money."* And it refuses to pretend to police
everyone: *"We cannot control what every user does with our creations... we
retain the right to deny our endorsement."* That is the correct distinction
between **authority** (what we may bind) and **control** (what we cannot).

## 4. The Penny Clause as liturgy, not price

§14 asks for a **physical pre-1982 copper penny** as proof-of-read. It is
whimsical, but structurally it is not a fee:

- it is **not** a condition of use for the Populace (the Populace is free);
- it is a **token of acknowledgment**, deliberately inconvenient, physical,
  and non-scalable;
- it cannot be automated into a rent.

The theological reading is exact: a sacrament is an outward sign of an inward
reality, not a transaction. The Penny Clause is the licence's refusal to become
a subscription.

## 5. What was missing, and what this wave adds

The lineage had **identity, ownership, and governance** but no **normative
anthropology**: no account of what a person is for, and therefore no criterion
by which a rule is judged good rather than merely stable. G39 can tell you a
change did not regress; it cannot tell you the change was just.

The alignment wave adds that layer:

- **research/071** — the anthropology (imago Dei, freedom, conscience), the
  moral grammar (object/intention/circumstance), the political principles
  (common good, subsidiarity, solidarity, non-domination), and the Jesus
  principle (kenosis, agape, truth, mercy, jubilee, sabbath).
- **docs/ALIGNMENT-ARMOR.md + src/wubu_armor.c** — ten invariants as an
  executable, monotone refusal contract.

The licence says *who may use it and on what terms*. The armor says *what the
system may not do while using it*. G39 says *how the rules may change*.

## 6. Reading the timeline theologically

- The **identity purge** (2026-08-04) is a naming repentance: the work stopped
  carrying a name that was not its own.
- The **Brain/Body split** (2026-08-03) is a doctrine of distinct spheres: the
  brain learns, the body protects and acts; neither absorbs the other.
- The **dual evidence** (2026-08-09) is a conciliar principle: no single
  authority may redefine the floor; the golden suite is the rule of faith the
  live suite cannot overrule.
- The **alignment wave** (2026-10-18) is the recognition that a constitution
  without a doctrine of the person is only a machine for stability.
